# Smart Green House - Presentation Script

**For: Mikeyla, Grade 5, Gateway College Colombo**
**Event: IT Exhibition - Smart Country**

---

## Introduction

> Hello everyone! My name is Mikeyla from Grade 5.

> Welcome to my Smart Country project - the **Smart Green House**!

---

## What is it?

> In my Smart Country, we grow our own food. But farmers cannot watch every plant
> all day and all night.

> Sometimes plants die because nobody remembered to water them. And sometimes
> people give them **too much** water, which is also bad.

> My Smart Green House is a garden that **looks after itself**.

---

## How does it work?

> There is a **soil sensor** pushed into the soil. It measures how thirsty the plant is.

> There are **3 lights** that tell you how the plant feels:

> 1. **GREEN** - The plant is happy! The soil has enough water.
> 2. **YELLOW** - The soil is getting dry. The plant is a little thirsty.
> 3. **RED** - The soil is too dry! The plant needs water now.

> When the light turns red, the **pump turns on by itself** and waters the plant.

> When the soil is wet again, the pump stops and you hear a **happy beep**.

---

## Let me show you!

> *(Point at the green light)*

> Right now the light is **green** - my plant is happy.

> *(Take the probe out of the wet soil, or push it into the dry pot)*

> Watch - the soil is getting dry... now it is **yellow**...

> And now it is **red** - the plant is very thirsty!

> Listen... *(beep)* ...the pump switched on all by itself. It is watering the plant!

> Now the soil is wet again - the pump stopped, and the light is **green** again.

> Nobody had to press a single button.

---

## The safety rule

> I also gave my greenhouse a safety rule.

> The pump is **never** allowed to run for more than 5 seconds at a time. Then it
> waits 10 seconds for the water to soak into the soil before checking again.

> That way the plant never gets flooded, and no water is wasted.

---

## What did I use?

> - A **Magicbit NEO** board - a smart computer made in Sri Lanka!
> - A **soil moisture sensor** to feel how dry the soil is
> - A **water pump** and a **relay** to switch it on and off
> - **3 LEDs** and a **buzzer** to show how the plant feels

---
